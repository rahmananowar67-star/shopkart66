1. About ShopKart

ShopKart is an online tool that lets small shop owners ("Shop Owners") create a simple online shop, upload products, share a shop link (for example in an Instagram bio) and receive orders from their customers ("Customers"). By creating an account or placing an order you agree to these terms.

2. ShopKart is a platform, not the seller

Products are listed, priced, packed, delivered and supported by the Shop Owner. ShopKart does not sell products, does not hold stock and is not a party to the sale between a Shop Owner and a Customer. Questions about a product, delivery, replacement or refund must be settled with the Shop Owner.

  • You must not sell illegal, counterfeit, stolen or prohibited items, or use the service to cheat Customers.
  • You must deliver orders you accept within the delivery time you have shown, and handle Customer data only to fulfil orders.
  • We may suspend a shop that breaks these terms or the law.

4. Customers and orders

When you place an order, the details you enter (name, phone number, address and optional location) are shared with the Shop Owner so they can deliver it. Payment is made as shown in the order form (Cash on Delivery, or UPI where the Shop Owner has enabled it). Delivery times are estimates given by the Shop Owner.

5. Plans and fees

Every new shop currently gets a free plan of 30 days, and no payment is collected at present. In future ShopKart may offer paid plans. Before any paid plan starts, Shop Owners will be told the price and what is included, and no charge will be made without their action. When a free plan ends, the shop may be paused until the Shop Owner contacts us to continue. Their data is not deleted because a plan ended.

6. Refunds and cancellation

No fees are being collected now, so there is nothing to refund. This section will be updated with a clear refund and cancellation policy before any paid plan begins. Refunds for products bought by Customers are decided by the Shop Owner who sold them.
