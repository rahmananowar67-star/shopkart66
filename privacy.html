Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

1. What we collect

  • Shop Owners: email, password (stored in encrypted form), shop name, business WhatsApp number, optional UPI ID, products, photos, prices and stock.
  • Customers: when you place an order: name, phone number, delivery address, city, pincode, optional location, and the items ordered.

2. How we use it

  • To run the shop and show products to Customers.
  • To send an order to the Shop Owner so they can deliver it, and to let a Customer see the status of their own orders.
  • To keep the service secure, fix problems and contact Shop Owners about their account.

We do not sell personal data and do not show ads.

3. Who can see your data

A Customer's order details are visible to the Shop Owner of the shop where the order was placed and to nobody else. A Shop Owner's shop name, products and shop contact details are public on their shop page. Our service providers (for example our database and hosting providers) store data on our behalf.

4. On your device

We keep a sign-in session for Shop Owners, and on a Customer's phone we remember order IDs so the "Mere Orders" list works. This is stored in your browser and can be cleared in your browser settings.

5. Keeping data

We keep data while an account is active or as needed to run orders. You can ask us to delete your account or your order details at any time.

6. Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you by writing to the email below. Shop Owners can edit or delete their products at any time from the dashboard.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect data, but no online service is completely secure. Keep your password private.

8. Children

ShopKart is not meant for children under 18 to open a Shop Owner account.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
